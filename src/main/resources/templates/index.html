01Import your stops

Choose route screenshots From Photos or Files · PNG, JPEG, and HEIC

On iPhone, choose Photo Library or Browse/Choose Files. PNG, JPEG, and HEIC images work directly; HEIC is converted automatically.

Images are processed on the RouteListToTesla server by three local OCR engines: Tesseract, PaddleOCR, and EasyOCR. They are not uploaded to an AI provider. Review or edit extracted addresses before sending the route to your Tesla.
